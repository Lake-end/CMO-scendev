1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Operation KOKKO is the national-level Dispersal and Survivability Maneuver of the Finnish Defence Forces. Phase 1 is concluded; 95% of assets are at Maximum Combat Readiness (MCR).

2. OPERATIONAL PHASES (LOE)

  • LOE 1 (ACE): Dispersal to Highway Strips and provincial airfields.
  • LOE 2 (NAV): Rapid Sortie to littoral camouflage zones.
  • LOE 3 (CIP): ASRAD-R/NASAMS II deployment to Greater Helsinki Area.
  • LOE 4 (IKU-TURSO): Åland fortification & A2/AD initiation.

5. IMMEDIATE ACTION

Fighter aircraft must maintain Full Readiness for Displacement. Units shall remain at Home Bases until the designated "GO" word is issued.

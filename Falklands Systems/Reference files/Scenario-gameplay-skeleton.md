# Command: Modern Operations (CMO) Falklands 2027 Scenario Gameplay Skeleton

---

## Overview of gameplay from players persepective (initial design and ideas)

### Starting situation 
* Gameplay starts in boxing day 2027, Falkalands have been taken by suprise action by Argentina utilizing assymmetric methods, especially massive drone strikes from civilian/merchant vessels surrounding the islands and followed by multiple landings of troops utlizing fishing ships and later large civilian ro-ro ferries to transport heavy equipment.
* Drones managed to overwhelm the UK airdefenses in matter of minutes and destroy the launchers, radars and even take out the RAF fighter detachment trying to scramble. The runway was also cratered by bombing runs made by Argentinian F-16´s after the airdefenses, radars and jets were taken out.
* UK Goverment (MoD?) orders immediate deployment of assets to recover the islands
* UK has only limited assets available at short notice during the holiday period, there is only Queen Elizabeth carrier, one Type 45 destroyer, one Type 23 Frigate and RFA Oiler ready to sail immediately. In theater there is sole HMS Medway OPV which has been ordered monitor the situation around the islands but avoid engagement. RAF also has limited aircraft ready to support the liberation effort on short notice.

### First steps for player
* When scenario loads a CTFS UI opens to the player presenting optional forces and "boons" player can purchase by delaying sailing to Falklands and gathering more forces. The longer player delays his departure the stronger the Argentinian grip on islands gets.
* After player has purchased additional forces (or chosen not to purchase anything) scenario clock is moved forward matching amount, player forces are spawned on the map on or near British Isles. After this a new Orders window is displayed for player instructing him to construct his fleet smartly, send (possible) subs directly Falklands at best speed and other fleet to head to Ascension island for refuel and resupply before continuing towards Falklands.
* In this phase player is required to complete or start these tasks:
    * Create fleets and sail them towards Ascension, slow ships should be grouped together and be still protected by an escort. CSG should sail best speed taking into account fuel usage and available tanker support.
    * Start the airbridge between UK and Ascension island. C-17 Globemasters: ammo for troops and helicopters, maybe some supplies for the carrier, spare parts and maintenance/repair equipment for aircraft and helicopter, airfield equipment to extend operations and medical facilities, airport support vehicles. A400M´s: pallets of rations, ammunition, water, basic supplies for troops also Container Delivery Systems for airdrops and paratroop equipment, also light vehicles like Jackal/Coyote, quads, trailers, bikes, rapid refueler systems to suppor landing zones. Few Typhoons are assigned for Ascension guard duty and must be flown in from the UK.

### Theatre Forward Presence
* Setup (possible) submarines player has bought to patrol around Falklands. Start sorting the naval traffic by sonar and try to find the Argentinian warships.
* Argentinian ASW planes are patrolling the area and can cause issues.
    
### Midway (Ascension)
* When reaching Ascension new orders are given to player. Player should organize a combat air patrol over the Ascension to protect against possible drone attacks from neutral shipping and organise ASW patrols to protect the fleet from submarine attacks. The fleet must be organized for attacking the islands and make landings. Also continuing air and sea bridges from UK to Ascension needs to be kept running.
* Fleet gathers in Ascension, just like 1982 to reorganise stores and equipment for combat landings, refuel and resuply. Troops will embark to amphibious assault ships and prepare for combat landings. This will take 2-4 days to complete hastily.
* Intel update on the islands is delivered to the player, including the news of surrender of British garrison on Falklands.

### Falklands Approaches (about 600nm out)
* Player needs to start missions by ISR platforms (Wedgetail, Rivet Joint, P-8s and long range UAVS) to gather intelligence picture of islands and surroundings. These missions provide bonuses for the GCE and provide targets for the F-35s. 
* Argentinian submarine threat is possible in this phase.
* SITREP event is enabled and gives GCE updates every 6 hours.
* Argentinian FACs could cause issues, maybe armed with Gabriel V missiles
* Argentinian F-16s can shoot down ISR platforms if they venture too close to the islands.
* Argentinian can detect players fleet with commercial satellite support


### Falklands AoO (within 300nm of the islands)
* Carrier can now start strike missions against enemy ships and facilities.
* Argentinian F-16s, submarines, FACs and SSM batteries on shore will all be a serious threat to players fleet.
* Argentinian forces can detect players fleets via satellites and F-16 radars or P-3 Orions.
* Player needs to setup ISR missions (drones, AEWC, ELINT, Poseidonds etc) with two aims: give GCE bonuses and locate the enemy ships, subs and ground targets.
* CAP missions are required to protect the fleets and also the ISR assets. If gap in defenses is detected then AI tries to exploit it.

### Falklands landings
* Selected landing points need to be cleared of sea mines. Bay-class ships act as motherships to unmanned minesweepers.
* Amphibious ships need to be escorted close to shore in few selected landing beaches.
* Landings need to be supported by helicopters, naval gunfire and air suppport and also by drones for ISR and targeting.
* Argentinian forces will try to counter with artillery, SSMs, rockets, helicopters and F-16 strikes and by moving forces in GCE engine.

### Capturing the islands
* GCE handles combat over the important zones on the islands and it abstracts actual ground combat.
* Player can influence the outcome by providing support, more troops via airdrops, helicopters and landings. Also several bonuses can give edge on the control fight over zones. (see GCE code)
* AI tries to react and give player hard time by shifting forces, reinforcing and spawning more threats for players units (SAMs, SSMs airstrikes etc)

### Endgame
* After enough ground is captured and most of the Argentinian forces are eliminated or surrendering a new SITREP event is displayed to player.
* A cool summary of your campaign over the Falklands War 2027 is generated for player which shows losses, kills and other interesting statistics about the campaign. This will be HTML bases interactive report. Draw inspiration from game "Sea Power" and its new dynamic campaign Pacific Strike 85.

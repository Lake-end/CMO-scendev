# UK Task Force Selector - points/time cost — Falklands 2027

---

## 0. Basics
* Costs are in points which need to be converted to a time cost.
* Player can use 100 points until which the mission becomes impossible due to time delay
* The more points player uses, the longer mission start is delayed and the stronger the Argentine gets
* Ground forces have no costs and dont need to be bought
* Frigates, Destroyers and other ships that have facilities will have free helicopters and UAVs for free.
